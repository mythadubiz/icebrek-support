❓ FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?
Go to Settings (gear icon) → scroll to "Restore Purchases" and tap it. Your premium access will be restored instantly.


How do I change the language?
Go to Settings → Language → select from 17 available languages. Cards translate in real time.


How do I enable or disable categories?
Go to Settings → scroll to Categories → toggle the categories you want on or off.


Is my data collected?
No personal data. Your cards, favorites and settings stay on your device. Since version 1.18, IceBrek can send anonymous usage statistics — only if you accept when asked, and you can switch them off in Settings. No ad tracking. See the privacy policy.
